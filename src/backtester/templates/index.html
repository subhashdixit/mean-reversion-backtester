03 / METHOD

A signal is not an executable return

Past ratios only→Signal after close t→Trade at close t+1→Earn return t+1 to t+2

The rule buys when close/SMA falls below a rolling past-only quantile, exits to cash above the upper quantile and otherwise keeps its prior signal. Exposure is delayed by a full bar. This reduces look-ahead errors, but does not establish a profitable strategy.

Changing settings repeatedly after viewing the same history can overfit that history. This is exploratory analysis, not a separately validated forecast. Price adjustment choices and annualization frequency affect the answer.
